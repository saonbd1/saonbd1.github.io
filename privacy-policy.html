Privacy Policy

What information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

Light SEO Tools respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details. If you submit a form or request a report, we collect the email address you provide.
  • Request details. The report type you select and any URL list or notes you submit.

Information we do not collect

The browser helpers run locally in your browser. The text and URLs you paste into those tools are processed on your device and are not transmitted to or stored by us.

How we use information

We use the information you submit only to process your request, deliver the requested report, and respond to your inquiries. We do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

To fulfill requests, your information may be processed by third-party services, including our form provider (Formspree) and report storage providers. These services receive only the information needed to perform their function.

Data retention

We retain request information only as long as needed to deliver the report and maintain records. Report files are delivered via private links and should be removed or rotated after their retention period.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. It may use basic analytics or functional services that store limited information in your browser to improve the experience.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. We will respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact page.
